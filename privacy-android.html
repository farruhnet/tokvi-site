Privacy Policy — Tokvi for Android

Effective November 18, 2026

In short: Tokvi for Android has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your recordings, photos and projects stay on your phone. We do not collect any personal data.

This policy explains how the Tokvi app for Android ("Tokvi", "we") handles information. Tokvi is developed by Farrukh Makhmudov. The iPhone app has its own privacy policy.

Camera and microphone

Tokvi uses the camera and microphone only while you record. Recordings are saved in the app's private storage on your device and are never uploaded to us.

Speech recognition and translation

To cut pauses, filler words and retakes and to create captions, Tokvi converts your speech to text on your device using the open-source Vosk engine. To translate captions, Tokvi uses Google ML Kit on-device translation. Neither feature sends your audio or text to us or to any server. The first time you use a language, the app downloads a language model: speech models come from alphacephei.com and translation models from Google. Those servers can see your IP address, as with any download, but receive none of your content.

Photos

If you allow it, Tokvi reads your photo library to suggest B-roll that matches what you say. Photos are analyzed on your device with Google ML Kit image labeling; the resulting labels are cached on your device and are never uploaded. Tokvi also saves finished videos to your gallery when you export them.

Music and files you import

Music you choose is copied into the project on your device and used only in your video.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Your purchase status is checked with Google Play on your device.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Tokvi for Android does not include analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell or share your data, because we do not collect it.

Data retention and deletion

All projects are stored on your device. You can delete a project inside the app, or delete all data by uninstalling the app. Videos you exported to your gallery remain there until you delete them.

Children

Tokvi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request on our support page.
