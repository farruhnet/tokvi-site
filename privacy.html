Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

In short: Retake has no accounts, no ads and no third-party tracking. Your recordings, photos and projects stay on your iPhone. We do not collect personal data.

This policy explains how the Retake app ("Retake", "we") handles information. Retake is developed by Farrukh Makhmudov.

Camera and microphone

Retake uses the camera and microphone only while you record. Recordings are saved in the app's private storage on your device and are never uploaded to us.

Speech recognition

To cut pauses, filler words and retakes and to create captions, Retake converts your speech to text using Apple's Speech framework. Whenever possible this happens entirely on your device. On some devices or for some languages, when an on-device model is not available, iOS may send the audio to Apple's speech recognition servers to transcribe it. That processing is performed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we never receive your audio or the resulting text.

Photos

If you allow it, Retake reads your photo library to suggest B-roll that matches what you say. Photos are analyzed on your device with Apple's Vision framework; the resulting labels are cached on your device and are never uploaded. Retake also asks for permission to add finished videos to your Photos.

Music and files you import

Music you choose from Files is copied into the project on your device and used only in your video.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Your purchase status is checked with Apple on your device.

Analytics and diagnostics

Retake does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. Anonymous technical events are written only to your device's system log. If you have enabled sharing with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports.

Data retention and deletion

All projects are stored on your device. You can delete a project inside the app, or delete all data by removing the app. Videos you exported to Photos remain there until you delete them.

Children

Retake is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request on our support page.
