Support

Retake — record yourself talking to the camera and get a ready-to-post video in minutes.

Need help or found a bug?

Open a request on our support page. Describe what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version, and attach a screenshot if you can. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

Speech recognition doesn't work for my language.
Retake uses Apple's speech recognition. Make sure the language is set correctly when you create a video and that your iPhone is connected to the internet the first time, so iOS can download the language model.

How do I change the app language?
Tap the globe icon on the home screen, or open Settings → Retake → Language.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Retake Pro → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged again.

I bought Pro but it isn't active.
Open the Pro screen in Retake and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Where are my videos stored?
Only on your iPhone. Finished videos are saved to your Photos when you choose to export them. Deleting the app deletes its projects.
